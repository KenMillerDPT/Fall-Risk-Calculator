🔬 Next Step: In-Depth Examination

When screening identifies elevated fall risk, the 2025 APTA Geriatrics CPG recommends a comprehensive PT examination to identify which balance subdomains are impaired — guiding individualized intervention selection.

Mini-BESTest
14-item balance evaluation across anticipatory, reactive, sensory orientation, and dynamic gait domains. Age-standardized cut-offs now available (Magnani et al., 2024). Identifies which subdomain is driving impairment.
BESTest (full)
36-item comprehensive balance evaluation across 6 subdomains. More detailed than Mini-BESTest; useful for complex presentations. Both tools go beyond fall risk screening to guide treatment selection.

⚠ Neither the Mini-BESTest nor the BESTest has published +LR/−LR values sufficient for inclusion in the Bayesian PoTP chain. They are examination tools, not screening tools, and cannot be added to this calculator.
